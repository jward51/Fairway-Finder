Fairway Finder SD — Cloudflare Pages iPhone deploy

1. Save the ZIP to Files on iPhone.
2. In Safari open https://dash.cloudflare.com and sign in.
3. Workers & Pages > Create application > Get started > Drag and drop your files (wording may vary).
4. Name project fairway-finder-sd or another unique name.
5. Choose the ZIP and Deploy site.
6. Open the resulting https://YOUR-PROJECT.pages.dev URL in Safari.
7. Safari Share > Add to Home Screen.

This prototype has no live tee-time feeds or automated notifications. GPS and saved searches are device-local. JC Golf booking opens its official CPS portal and may reuse an existing Safari session. No passwords are stored.
